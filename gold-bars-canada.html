What to Check Before Buying

Gold Bar Buying Checklist

  • Compare the premium over the current gold spot price
  • Confirm refinery, fineness, serial number and assay packaging
  • Review payment fees and minimum order requirements
  • Verify insured shipping, signature requirements and delivery timing
  • Check storage options and buyback policies

Recognized Refiners and Mints

Recognition matters because it can affect resale confidence and dealer acceptance. Buyers commonly compare products from established refiners and sovereign mints, but availability changes. Always verify the exact product, assay card and dealer terms before purchase.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are gold bars better than gold coins?

Bars can be more premium-efficient, while coins may offer stronger recognizability and smaller resale units. The better choice depends on budget, storage and resale flexibility.

Do gold bars come with certificates?

Many smaller bars are sold in tamper-evident assay packaging showing weight, purity and serial details. Verify the exact product before buying.

Where should gold bars be stored?

Common options include a secured home safe, a bank safe-deposit box and professional allocated vault storage.
