Why self-employed files get declined

Most declines aren't really about whether you can afford the mortgage. They're about how the file was put together.

Write-offs that saved you tax also cut your income on paper
A file sent to the wrong kind of lender first
A down year, or a one-time expense, with no explanation
A small CRA balance owing that nobody mentioned
Missing NOAs, financial statements or HST returns that stall the underwriter
Dividends, retained earnings and add-backs that were never put in front of the lender

Questions

Will this get me approved?

No product can promise approval. Lenders make their own decisions. The kit helps you understand how your file will be read, fix what you can, and present everything completely, so you're assessed on your real situation.

Does it work for incorporated business owners?

Yes. It covers salary plus dividends, dividend gross-ups, retained earnings, and the corporate documents lenders ask for.

I've been self-employed for less than two years. Is it still useful?

Yes. The guide explains the stated-income route, how to write a transition letter, and a roadmap for refinancing to a lower-cost lender once you have two full years filed.

Is this Canada-specific?

Yes. It's built around CRA documents (T1, T2125, NOAs), Canadian lender channels, and the federal stress test.

Is this mortgage or tax advice?

No. It's an educational preparation kit. Talk to a licensed mortgage professional and your accountant before you make financing or tax decisions.
