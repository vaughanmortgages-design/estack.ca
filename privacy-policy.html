Privacy Policy

eStack.ca collects only the information needed to operate the site, respond to financing requests, measure performance and connect users with relevant providers when appropriate.

Information we collect

  • Basic website analytics such as page views, device/browser information, referral source and general location.
  • Information you voluntarily submit through the Funding Matcher, such as name, email, mobile number, province, financing category, requested amount range, urgency and broad business or credit information.
  • Marketing attribution such as UTM source, medium and campaign, plus affiliate-link click activity.

eStack does not ask for your SIN, bank-account login, exact credit score or date of birth in the Funding Matcher.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to your request, identify a relevant financing category or partner route, improve the matcher, prevent abuse, measure marketing performance and operate eStack.ca.

Consent and contact

When you submit the Funding Matcher, you must actively consent to being contacted regarding your financing request and, where relevant, being connected with financing providers. You may withdraw future marketing/contact consent by emailing info@estack.ca.

Sharing

We may share the information you submit with a financing provider when that is relevant to the request you asked us to help with. We do not sell a general list of Funding Matcher submissions to unrelated advertisers.

Storage and retention

Form submissions may be stored by eStack's website-hosting and form-processing providers. We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the request, recordkeeping, fraud prevention, legal obligations and legitimate business analysis, then delete or anonymize it when appropriate.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No internet system is guaranteed to be perfectly secure, so do not send highly sensitive credentials or banking information through ordinary website forms or email.

Third-party links

eStack.ca links to third-party providers. Their privacy policies and terms apply after you leave eStack. Review them before submitting additional personal information.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: info@estack.ca.
