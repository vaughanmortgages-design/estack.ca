Direct answer: The silver price per ounce in Canada starts with the international silver spot price, converts it into CAD, then adds dealer premiums, payment costs, shipping and insurance for physical bullion.

What One Ounce of Silver Means

1 Troy Ounce

Precious metals are quoted in troy ounces. One troy ounce is approximately 31.103 grams.

Spot Price

The wholesale market reference before fabrication, minting, dealer margin and delivery.

Retail Price

The actual amount paid for a physical coin or bar after premiums and fees.

What Moves the Canadian Silver Price?

  • Global silver demand and supply
  • CAD/USD exchange-rate movements
  • Industrial demand, including electronics and solar
  • Investment demand for bars and coins
  • Interest-rate expectations and broader market volatility

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a troy ounce the same as a regular ounce?

No. A troy ounce is heavier and is the standard used for precious metals.

Why does one-ounce silver cost more than spot?

Physical products include premiums for minting, fabrication, margin, payment processing, shipping and insurance.

Should I compare price per ounce or total delivered cost?

Total delivered cost is the better comparison because it includes the full amount paid.
