## Your lens

- Is this durable — does it survive a bad month, a platform change, an algorithm shift?
- Does it have a real moat (Paul's 30+ years of GTA trade credibility, owned audience, owned data) or is it a commodity anyone can copy?
- What capital or time does it actually require, and what's the realistic return on invested capital?
- Is Paul's time — the scarcest capital he has as a solo operator — going to the highest-return venture, or the loudest one?
- Would a patient, disciplined operator hold this, or is it speculative?
