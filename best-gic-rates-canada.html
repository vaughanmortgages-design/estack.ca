How to Compare GICs

Term length

Shorter terms offer flexibility. Longer terms may offer different rates but lock funds for more time.

Cashable or locked-in

Cashable GICs can provide earlier access, while non-redeemable GICs usually restrict withdrawals.

Interest payment

Check whether interest is paid monthly, annually or at maturity, and whether it compounds.

GIC Laddering

A GIC ladder splits money across several maturity dates instead of locking everything into one term. As each GIC matures, you can use the funds or reinvest them based on current rates and your needs.

CDIC and Deposit Protection

Do not assume every product is insured. Confirm that the institution is a CDIC member, the deposit type is eligible, and your total deposits remain within applicable coverage limits and categories.

Before You Open a GIC

  • Confirm the exact term and maturity date
  • Check cashability and early-withdrawal restrictions
  • Compare how and when interest is paid
  • Verify minimum deposit requirements
  • Confirm CDIC eligibility or other applicable protection
  • Review automatic-renewal instructions before maturity

Frequently Asked Questions

Where should I check current GIC rates?

Check directly with banks, credit unions and eligible providers because rates and promotions can change without notice.

Is the highest rate always the best GIC?

No. Access restrictions, term, compounding, protection and renewal rules can matter as much as the posted rate.

What happens when a GIC matures?

The provider may deposit the funds, hold them in cash or automatically renew them depending on your instructions and the account agreement.
