# Risk Framework — [Venture/Decision]

Used by the Dalio (Risk) seat and as a standalone pre-launch checklist.

## Regulatory / compliance
- [ ] Does this touch FSRA-licensed mortgage activity? If yes, does it stay inside Vaughan Mortgage Group and out of estack.ca?
- [ ] Does this touch AGCO-regulated gambling content? If yes, does it stay inside TrueNorth Bets with required disclosures?
- [ ] Any affiliate disclosure requirements (Amazon, Gumroad, KDP) unmet?
- [ ] Any platform ToS risk (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Shopify) that could get an account suspended?

## Brand separation
- [ ] Does this blur a boundary Paul has deliberately kept separate between brands?
- [ ] Is the right persona/brand voice being used (Vaughan Mortgage Group vs. Ray Cutter vs. Little Lamb, etc.)?

## Competitive / market
- [ ] What happens if a competitor copies this in 90 days?
- [ ] Is this dependent on one channel, one algorithm, or one platform's goodwill?

## Financial
- [ ] What's the worst-case capital loss if this fails outright?
- [ ] What's the time cost, and what else isn't getting done while this runs?

## Macro / second-order
- [ ] What changes if interest rates, ad costs, or a platform policy shift against this?
- [ ] What's the early warning sign that this is failing, and who's watching for it?

## Verdict
- **Risk level:** Low / Medium / High
- **Guardrail to add before shipping:** [one line]
