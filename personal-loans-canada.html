Personal Loan Checklist

  • Borrow only what you need and can reasonably repay
  • Compare APR, fees and total repayment amount
  • Confirm whether payments are weekly, biweekly or monthly
  • Check late-payment, NSF and early-repayment terms
  • Read the consent language before authorizing a credit check
  • Keep copies of the agreement and payment schedule

Frequently Asked Questions

Does comparing personal loans guarantee approval?

No. Every provider makes its own eligibility and approval decision.

Should I choose the lowest payment?

Not automatically. A lower payment can come from a longer term and may increase the total cost.

Can rates and fees change?

Yes. Verify the current rate, fees and full agreement directly with the provider before accepting an offer.
