Before You Apply

  • Set a realistic vehicle budget
  • Compare total borrowing cost, not only the monthly payment
  • Review the loan term and payment frequency
  • Check fees, down-payment requirements and penalties
  • Confirm whether the rate is fixed or variable
  • Read the provider's approval and refinancing conditions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get an auto loan with challenged credit?

Some providers consider a range of credit profiles, but eligibility, rates and approval are determined by the provider. No outcome is guaranteed.

Is auto-loan refinancing always worthwhile?

No. Compare the remaining balance, new term, fees and total repayment amount before proceeding.

Are powersports loans the same as car loans?

They can have different qualification rules, terms and collateral requirements. Review the provider's current information carefully.
