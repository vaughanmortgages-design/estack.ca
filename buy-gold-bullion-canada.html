What Does “Premium Over Spot” Mean?

The spot price is the market reference price for gold. A retail buyer usually pays more than spot because the final cost can include fabrication, dealer margin, payment processing, shipping and insurance. Compare the total delivered cost, not only the advertised premium.

Buying Gold From CIBC or Another Bank

Canadians often search for whether CIBC or another major bank sells gold. Some financial institutions offer precious metals or access to bullion products, but product availability and terms can change. Before buying through a bank, compare its current selection, minimum purchase, pickup or delivery method, premium and storage options with specialist bullion dealers.

Important: A familiar bank name does not automatically mean the lowest all-in bullion cost. Compare the exact product, weight, purity and final delivered price.

How to Buy Physical Gold in Canada Safely

  • Verify the exact weight and purity, such as 1 oz and 99.99% fine gold where applicable.
  • Check the mint or refiner and any assay or packaging information.
  • Compare card, bank-transfer and wire-payment pricing.
  • Confirm shipping is tracked, insured and signature-required.
  • Review return and buyback policies.
  • Keep invoices and product documentation.

Storage Options in Canada

Home safe

Consider physical security, discreet installation and whether your insurance specifically covers bullion.

Bank safe-deposit box

Ask about access hours, box availability and insurance. The contents of a safe-deposit box are not the same as an insured bank deposit.

Professional vault storage

Confirm whether storage is allocated or unallocated, how ownership is documented, insurance coverage, audit practices and withdrawal fees.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I buy physical gold in Canada?

Use an established dealer, coin shop or institution, compare the all-in price and verify product, delivery and storage details before paying.

Are gold bars or coins better?

Coins are often easier to resell in smaller amounts. Bars may be more premium-efficient for larger purchases.

Does Scotiabank or CIBC sell gold?

Precious-metals availability at Canadian banks can change. Check the institution's current offering and compare its total cost with specialist dealers.
