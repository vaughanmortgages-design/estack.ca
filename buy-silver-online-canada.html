Understand the Premium Over Spot

The spot price is the market reference price for silver. Retail buyers normally pay more than spot because the final price can include fabrication, dealer margin, payment processing, shipping and insurance.

Smaller coins and bars often carry a higher percentage premium than larger bars. A lower advertised premium is not necessarily the lowest total cost once fees are included.

Payment, Delivery and Authenticity

  • Payment: Confirm whether card, bank transfer or other methods change the final price.
  • Delivery: Check whether shipping is tracked, insured and signature-required.
  • Product details: Verify weight, purity, mint or refiner, packaging and assay information.
  • Seller: Review established history, contact details and written policies.
  • Records: Keep invoices and product documentation.

Storage & Delivery Checklist

  • Confirm insured shipping and signature requirements before payment.
  • Verify how damaged or missing shipments are handled.
  • Decide where the silver will be stored before ordering.
  • Keep invoices and product details for insurance and resale records.

Storage Options in Canada

Home safe

Consider physical security, discreet installation, access controls and whether your insurer specifically covers bullion.

Bank safe-deposit box

Ask about access hours, box availability and insurance. Contents are not the same as an insured bank deposit.

Professional vault storage

Confirm whether storage is allocated or unallocated, how ownership is documented, insurance coverage, audit practices and withdrawal fees.

Buyback Considerations

Before purchasing, review whether the dealer publishes buyback terms, how resale prices are calculated, which products they accept and whether shipping or authentication fees apply.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best way to buy silver in Canada?

Compare established dealers using total delivered cost, premium over spot, payment fees, insured delivery, authenticity information and buyback terms.

Are silver coins or silver bars better?

Coins can offer recognizability and smaller resale units, while bars can be more premium-efficient in larger sizes.

How should silver bullion be stored?

Common options include a secured home safe, bank safe-deposit box and professional allocated vault storage.
