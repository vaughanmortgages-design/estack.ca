How the Calculation Works

1. Convert weight

The tool converts grams, kilograms or regular ounces into troy ounces.

2. Apply purity

The selected karat or fineness adjusts the amount of pure gold in the item.

3. Multiply by price

Pure-gold troy ounces are multiplied by the CAD price per troy ounce you entered.

4. Compare offers

Dealer offers may be lower than melt value because of spreads, testing and handling costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this calculator use a live gold price?

No. You enter the current price yourself so the estimate cannot silently use an outdated feed.

Is melt value the same as resale value?

No. A resale or dealer offer may be lower after testing, refining, handling and dealer spreads.

Why are troy ounces used?

Precious metals are commonly quoted in troy ounces. One troy ounce equals approximately 31.1034768 grams.
