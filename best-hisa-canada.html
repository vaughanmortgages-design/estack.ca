Best High-Interest Savings Accounts in Canada (HISA Guide 2026)

Reviewed August 2026 · estack.ca editorial team
Direct answer: The best HISA in Canada depends on more than the headline rate. Compare the regular rate after any promotion, fees, minimum balance, access to your money, deposit-protection eligibility and whether you want the account inside a TFSA.

High-interest savings accounts can be useful for emergency funds, short-term goals and cash you do not want exposed to market risk. Because HISA rates change frequently, this page focuses on the comparison factors that stay useful even when a bank changes its advertised rate.

Everyday Rate vs Promotional HISA Rate

This is one of the biggest traps when comparing savings accounts. A promotional HISA rate may look like the highest-interest option in Canada, but it can last only a few months or apply only to new deposits. The better comparison is the total interest you expect to earn over the period you plan to keep the money there.

Quick rule: Write down the promotional rate, the date it ends and the regular HISA rate that follows. That makes different offers much easier to compare.

Simplii Financial HISA and Other Promotional Offers

Simplii Financial is frequently searched by Canadians looking for promotional high-interest savings offers. Like other banks that use promotional pricing, the exact offer can change by customer, deposit type and campaign period. Check the current terms directly before moving money, and compare the post-promotion rate as carefully as the headline offer.

The same approach applies to Tangerine and other providers that periodically use temporary savings promotions: compare the full offer period, not just the largest percentage shown in an ad.

Digital Banks and Everyday HISA Options

Providers such as EQ Bank, Wealthsimple and Oaken are commonly considered by Canadians who prefer a competitive everyday savings rate without constantly moving money between promotions. Product structures differ, so compare fees, access, registered-account availability and deposit-protection details rather than assuming every high-interest account works the same way.

How Much Difference Can a Better HISA Rate Make?

Even a modest rate difference matters as balances grow. For example, a one-percentage-point difference on $25,000 is roughly $250 of additional gross interest over a full year before tax, assuming the rate and balance stay unchanged.

HISA vs TFSA

A HISA describes the type of savings product; a TFSA is a registered tax wrapper. Some financial institutions let you hold a high-interest savings product inside a TFSA. If you have available contribution room, that can allow the interest to grow tax-free. Confirm current CRA contribution limits and your own available room before contributing.

HISA checklist before opening an account

  • Check the current regular rate.
  • Check whether the advertised rate is promotional.
  • Record the promotion end date and eligibility rules.
  • Check fees, minimum balances and transfer limits.
  • Confirm deposit-protection eligibility.
  • Decide whether you need a regular account or TFSA HISA.
  • Verify the latest terms directly with the financial institution.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a HISA in Canada?

A HISA is a high-interest savings account designed to pay a higher savings rate than many standard savings accounts while keeping funds accessible under the account's terms.

What is the best HISA rate in Canada?

There is no permanent single best rate because financial institutions change everyday and promotional rates frequently. Compare the current regular rate, any temporary promotion and the conditions attached to each offer.

Are promotional HISA rates worth it?

They can be, especially for larger balances, but only if the eligibility rules fit your situation and you know what rate applies after the promotion ends.
