Check these five things before buying.

  1. Spot versus retail price: calculate the actual premium you are paying.
  2. Bars versus coins: compare recognizability, size and resale convenience.
  3. Payment method: card, wire and other payment types may have different costs.
  4. Shipping and insurance: verify delivery costs and coverage.
  5. Storage: decide where the metal will be stored before it arrives.
