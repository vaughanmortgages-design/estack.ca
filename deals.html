Use the short list properly

Three rules for every click.

01

Confirm the live terms

Pricing, eligibility, availability and features can change. The provider page is the final source.

02

Compare alternatives

A featured partner is a starting point, not proof that it is the best fit for every person.

03

Know the objective

Choose the tool, lender or dealer that solves a defined need—not the one with the loudest promise.
