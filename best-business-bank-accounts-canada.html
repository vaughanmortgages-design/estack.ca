Direct answer: The best business bank account is the one with the lowest total cost for how your business actually gets paid, moves money, deposits funds and connects to bookkeeping tools.

What to Compare

Monthly cost

Compare the base fee together with included transactions and overage charges.

Payments and transfers

Check e-Transfer limits, bill payments, wires, merchant deposits and payment integrations.

Cash and branch access

Businesses handling cash should compare deposit limits, branch availability and night-deposit options.

Before Opening an Account

  • Estimate monthly deposits, withdrawals and transfers
  • Check included transactions and overage pricing
  • Review cash-deposit, ATM and wire fees
  • Confirm access for partners, bookkeepers and employees
  • Check accounting and payment integrations
  • Review foreign-exchange and cross-border costs

Separate Business and Personal Banking

A dedicated business account can make bookkeeping, tax preparation and cash-flow review cleaner. Requirements vary by legal structure and provider, so confirm what documents are needed before applying.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a free business bank account always best?

No. A no-fee account can cost more if transaction, deposit or transfer charges do not fit your activity.

What documents are usually required?

Providers commonly request identification and business-registration documents, but exact requirements vary.

Should I compare the advertised fee or total monthly cost?

Total monthly cost is the better measure because it includes overages, deposits, transfers, FX and other service charges.
