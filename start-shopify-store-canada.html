Step 1 — Sign Up and Pick a Plan

Shopify's free trial lets you build and test your store before you pay anything. Start with the Basic plan in CAD context if you want the most balanced launch option for a first store.

Step 2 — Set Up Canadian Taxes

This trips up a lot of new store owners. In Canada, tax rates vary by province — GST, HST, or PST depending on where your customer is located. Shopify handles this automatically if you configure taxes correctly and verify your registration requirements.

Step 3 — Activate Shopify Payments

Shopify Payments is available in Canada and can reduce friction at checkout. You'll need your business banking info and a Canadian address. Once activated, you can accept major payment methods and keep reconciliation simpler.

Step 4 — Configure Shipping

Set up domestic Canadian shipping rates first, then optionally US/international. Canada Post integration is built in. For high-volume operations, compare fulfillment tools before adding complexity.

Step 5 — Add Products and Launch

Keep it simple at launch. 5–10 products, clean photos, and clear descriptions. Focus on margin discipline in CAD after shipping, returns, and processing fees.

FAQ

How much does it cost to start a Shopify store in Canada?

Most new stores can launch on Shopify Basic with monthly platform fees in CAD plus payment processing and app costs. Your total depends on your plan, order volume, and shipping setup.

Do I need to charge GST/HST when selling online in Canada?

It depends on your registration status and where you ship. Shopify supports tax configuration by province, but you should confirm your obligations with CRA guidance or an accountant.

Can I use Shopify Payments in Canada?

Yes. Shopify Payments is available in Canada and can help simplify checkout and payouts. You will need eligible business information and banking details to activate it.
