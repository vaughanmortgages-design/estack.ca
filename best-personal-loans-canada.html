Before You Apply

  • Check your credit report and score
  • Compare APR, fees and total repayment
  • Confirm fixed or variable interest
  • Review prepayment and late-payment rules
  • Make sure the payment fits your monthly budget
  • Avoid applying with multiple lenders at once

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the lowest interest rate always the best loan?

No. Fees, term, payment frequency and total repayment can make a lower-rate loan more expensive overall.

Should I use pre-qualification first?

Where available, pre-qualification may help you review estimated terms before a full application.

Can I pay off a personal loan early?

Many lenders allow early repayment, but rules vary. Check for prepayment fees or restrictions.
