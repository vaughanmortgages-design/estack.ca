Before You Apply

  • Know the exact amount and purpose of the funding
  • Review recent revenue and cash-flow patterns
  • Compare total repayment, not only the advertised payment
  • Read all fees, guarantees and default terms
  • Confirm the repayment schedule fits normal business cash flow

Frequently Asked Questions

Will applying guarantee funding?

No. The provider decides eligibility, amount, pricing and terms.

What can business funding be used for?

Common uses include inventory, equipment, payroll timing, renovations, marketing and short-term working capital, subject to provider rules.

Should I choose the fastest option?

Not automatically. Speed can matter, but total cost and repayment pressure can matter more.
