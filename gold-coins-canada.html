What to Check Before Buying

Gold Coin Buying Checklist

  • Compare the premium over the current gold spot price
  • Verify mint, weight, purity and security features
  • Review payment fees and minimum order requirements
  • Confirm insured shipping and signature requirements
  • Check storage options and dealer buyback policies

Gold Coins vs Gold Bars

Coins may offer stronger recognizability and easier resale in smaller units. Bars can be more premium-efficient, especially in larger sizes. The better choice depends on budget, storage and resale flexibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Gold Maple Leafs a good choice for Canadian buyers?

They are widely recognized and highly pure, but buyers should still compare premiums, delivery and resale terms.

Are fractional gold coins worth it?

They offer smaller purchase and resale units, but usually carry a higher percentage premium.

How should gold coins be stored?

Common options include a secured home safe, a bank safe-deposit box and professional allocated vault storage.
