What to Check Before Buying

Silver Coin Buying Checklist

  • Compare the premium over the current silver spot price
  • Verify mint, weight, purity and security features
  • Review tube quantities, payment fees and minimum orders
  • Confirm insured shipping and signature requirements
  • Check storage options and dealer buyback policies

Silver Coins vs Silver Bars

Coins may offer stronger recognizability and easier resale in smaller units. Bars can be more premium-efficient, especially in larger sizes. The better choice depends on budget, storage and resale flexibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Silver Maple Leafs a good choice for Canadian buyers?

They are widely recognized and highly pure, but buyers should still compare premiums, delivery and resale terms.

Are tubes of silver coins worth it?

Tubes can simplify storage and reduce per-coin handling, but they require a larger upfront purchase.

How should silver coins be stored?

Common options include coin tubes, capsules, a secured home safe, a bank safe-deposit box and professional allocated vault storage.
