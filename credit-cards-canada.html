Before You Apply

  • Confirm the annual fee and all recurring costs
  • Review purchase, cash-advance and balance-transfer interest
  • Check eligibility and income requirements
  • Understand any security-deposit requirement
  • Verify foreign-transaction and late-payment fees
  • Read the provider’s full agreement before submitting

Credit Card vs. Secured Card

A traditional credit card generally does not require an upfront security deposit, while a secured card commonly does. A secured product may be considered by people building or rebuilding credit, but outcomes are never guaranteed and provider reporting practices should be verified directly.
