Does eStack approve business financing?

No. eStack is an information and referral platform. Providers make their own lending decisions.

What should I compare besides the payment?

Compare total repayment cost, payment frequency, term length, fees and any early-payoff conditions.

Can newer businesses explore options?

Yes, but availability varies significantly by provider and time in business. The matcher can route you to the most sensible starting point currently available.
